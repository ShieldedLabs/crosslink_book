# Mechanism Design Audit of Crosslink Zebra

**2025-12-11**

This design review audit was initiated as the Shielded Labs team approached the final stretch of their prototyping phase (see [the Roadmap](https://shieldedlabs.net/roadmap/) for the current status). This was useful timing between prototyping enough to have confidence the design is feasible for production versus too late in the process when the design is already "baked" to get a mechanism-design focused review of the consensus safety.

This audit focused on the core safety and reliability of Crosslink as a consensus foundation, and not on other crucial concerns like privacy, usability, the code implementation, and so on.

## Pre-Registration Process

Shielded Labs undertook a "pre-registration" process for this design audit by publicly committing to the scope and deliverables prior to engaging in the audit so that the public can verify we did not "shift the goal posts" after learning details from the auditor in order to influence the perception of the findings.

See the precommitted [Proposal](./2025-12-11-mech-design/proposal.md) for this audit.

## Results

The audit report is referenced by the [Crosslink Design Overview](../../crosslink-design-overview-20260914.md) as *Nikete's Mechanism Design Audit of Crosslink Zebra*, and several design choices there cite its recommendations (for example, items R2, R4, and R6 in [§14 Rewards](../../crosslink-design-overview-20260914.md#section-14)).
